# Player Segments Packet

## Simulation Summary

Gold Rush should satisfy overlapping player segments without becoming a generic live-service shooter.

## Segments

### Extraction Shooter Players

- Wants loot tension, risk, and meaningful extraction decisions.
- Failure sign: gold has shallow value or no loss pressure.
- Needed proof: gold zones, cargo risk, cashout, combat receipts.

### Battle Royale Players

- Wants readable match flow and quick stakes.
- Failure sign: too much inventory friction before fun.
- Needed proof: 2-100 player shards, readable routes, match phases.

### Survival/Sandbox Players

- Wants world identity, travel, towns, and resources.
- Failure sign: terrain is only a combat board.
- Needed proof: towns, rail, mountain, roads, gold sites.

### Western/Fantasy Players

- Wants frontier fantasy.
- Failure sign: no saloon/station/train/gold rush identity.
- Needed proof: town roles, train/lobby anchors, gold piles.

### Casual Co-op Players

- Wants low-friction team goals.
- Failure sign: harsh extraction loss without clarity.
- Needed proof: visible cashout goals and understandable risk.

## Recommended Next Action

Use role-specific HUD/debug readouts in development to prove each segment sees its core promise.
