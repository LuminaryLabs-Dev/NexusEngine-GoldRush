# Marketing Lead Packet

## Simulation Summary

A marketing lead will look for a one-line marketable promise, a visual hook, and a reason players would share this over other extraction shooters.

## Expected Outcome

- Hook: western gold-rush extraction battle royale where gold is score, ammo, health, weight, and loot.
- Visual hook: massive frontier terrain with towns, rail routes, mountains, and gold zones.
- Social hook: teams get greedy, ambushed, and forced to decide when to cash out.
- Differentiator: not military tactical looting; a frontier economy race.

## Failure Signs

- Positioning says only "battle royale" or "extraction shooter."
- Screenshots look generic.
- No clear player fantasy for TikTok/YouTube/Steam capsule copy.
- The game depends on unapproved legacy assets before it has a playable pitch.

## Evidence Needed

- Public Pages URL with a readable world screenshot.
- 10-second description and 30-second gameplay loop.
- Market packets showing audience and competitor risks.

## Recommended Next Action

Make the HUD and docs reinforce the pitch: gold pressure, town routes, cashout risk, and combat camera shift.
