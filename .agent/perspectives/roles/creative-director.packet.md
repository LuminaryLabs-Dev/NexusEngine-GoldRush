# Creative Director Packet

## Simulation Summary

A creative director should see a game identity forming around western extraction pressure: gold greed, frontier towns, dangerous travel, and a combat camera shift.

## Expected Outcome

- The first screenshot says "Gold Rush" before reading text.
- Terrain is massive and patch-based, not circular.
- Towns, rail, mountains, and gold zones create a western frontier silhouette.
- Gold is not just score; it is risk, ammo, carry weight, loot, and extraction pressure.
- Combat feels like a perspective change, not only a button state.

## Failure Signs

- The build looks like a generic battle royale/extraction prototype.
- The world has systems but no memorable landmarks.
- Towns are decorative and not tied to routes, gold, or cashout.
- Legacy Gold Rush concepts are hidden in docs only.

## Evidence Needed

- Browser screenshot with terrain, town silhouettes, routes, mountains, and HUD.
- Runtime proof of Mine -> Ambush -> Cash Out.
- Docs linking old Unity evidence to current design choices.

## Recommended Next Action

Make the next pass add stronger identity descriptors: town roles, gold-zone roles, route names, and combat/cashout readable states in `engine.n.*` snapshots and HUD.
