## Market Read

- The games market is still large and growing, but attention is concentrated.
- Shooter and battle royale engagement shows maturity after years of dominance.
- Extraction shooters are active but crowded.
- Recent extraction launches show both opportunity and risk: strong early revenue can be undermined by stability, server, and retention issues.
- Generic high-budget live-service extraction positioning is risky.

## Gold Rush Opportunity

- Western gold extraction is more distinctive than military looting.
- Gold can unify economy, ammo, health, weight, score, and social tension.
- Towns, rail, and terrain can create visual identity.
- 2-100 player shards allow a bigger-match promise without pretending one room owns all players.

## Market Risks

- Extraction fatigue: players may assume another gear-loss shooter.
- Battle royale fatigue: players may reject generic shrinking-map language.
- Live-service skepticism: players punish unstable launches.
- Asset trust: legacy/third-party western assets require provenance.
- Scope risk: "massive multiplayer" claims need narrow proof.

## Positioning Recommendation

Use this phrasing internally:

```txt
Gold Rush is a western extraction battle royale where every ounce of gold is score, ammo, weight, health risk, and the reason other players hunt you.
```

## Validation Need

The build should prove:

- 72 players -> 2 room shards.
- visible terrain/towns/routes/gold zones.
- mine -> ambush -> cashout.
- combat shifts camera/audio/animation state.
- public Pages build loads.
