# Storefront Positioning Packet

## Simulation Summary

A storefront viewer needs to understand genre, fantasy, risk loop, and visual identity before reading deep systems docs.

## Capsule Promise

```txt
Mine gold, carry the risk, survive the ambush, cash out before the frontier turns on you.
```

## Short Description Draft

```txt
Gold Rush is a western extraction battle royale for 2-100 players. Prospect across a massive frontier, fight over gold zones, move through towns and rail routes, and decide when to cash out. Gold is your score, your weight, your ammo pressure, and the reason every ambush matters.
```

## Screenshot Checklist

- Massive terrain with horizon blockers.
- Town or rail landmark visible.
- Gold zone readable.
- HUD shows carried/banked gold.
- Combat or cashout state visible.

## Failure Signs

- Store copy sounds like every other shooter.
- Screenshots are mostly UI/debug counters.
- No visible western iconography.
- No visible extraction/cashout goal.

## Recommended Next Action

Use public Pages screenshots as early internal marketing proof, but do not publish as final marketing until asset provenance is resolved.
