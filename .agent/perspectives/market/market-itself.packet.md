# Market Itself Packet

## Simulation Summary

The market will not reward Gold Rush for being another battle royale or extraction shooter; it needs a distinct fantasy and a stable proof loop.

## Expected Outcome

- The game is pitchable in one sentence.
- The first screenshot is not generic.
- The first interaction proves the core risk loop.
- The public build is stable and shareable.
- The scope is believable for the current stage.

## Failure Signs

- "Massive online multiplayer" without visible room/shard proof.
- "Battle royale" without a unique player fantasy.
- "Extraction shooter" without loot/economy depth.
- Prototype visuals that do not communicate western gold rush identity.
- Overpromising legacy asset parity before import gates complete.

## What The Market Rewards Here

- Strong concept compression.
- Distinct setting.
- Streamable greed moments.
- Visible technical credibility.
- Clear roadmap from prototype to asset-backed game.

## Recommended Next Action

Prioritize the playable gold economy and named-world readability over adding more generic shooter features.
