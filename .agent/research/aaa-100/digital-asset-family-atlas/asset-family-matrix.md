# Digital Asset Family Atlas

Status: active docs-only
Date: 2026-07-01
Domain: content / world / render / gameplay

## Purpose

Turn the current digital-asset gap into source-owned protokit families. The terrain source defines where content belongs; each asset family defines what forms, data, events, placement rules, provenance, and proof are needed before assets can replace primitives.

## Counts

- Asset families: 16
- Packets per family: 4
- Family packets: 64
- Paired research notes: 64

## Source Candidate Rule

Kenney, Quaternius, Poly Haven, OpenGameArt, and similar sources are candidates only. No candidate becomes runtime content without provenance, license review, sanitization, approval, source-to-runtime path proof, and browser-visible proof.
