# Research - VFX SFX And Diegetic Cues - Protokit Contract

Status: planned docs-only
Parent packet: 14-03-vfx-sfx-diegetic-cues-protokit-contract

## Research Question

What current source, market, art-direction, and proof knowledge should guide vfx sfx and diegetic cues - protokit contract?

## Source Signals

- Kenney offers thousands of free game assets and remains useful for lightweight toon/placeholder families.
- Quaternius provides many CC0 low-poly 3D models and glTF/OBJ/FBX-friendly packs that can inform toon protokit shape.
- Poly Haven provides CC0 HDRIs, textures, and models useful for lighting and material reference, though high-real scans need toon adaptation.
- OpenGameArt can provide CC0/community assets, but every item still needs per-asset license/provenance review before promotion.

## GoldRush Interpretation

VFX SFX And Diegetic Cues should be treated as a local GoldRush protokit family that consumes interaction, gold, extraction, combat state. It should not bypass source fixture masks, placement anchors, provenance gates, or human-view proof.

## Data Needed Before Import

- candidate source URL
- license text and license URL
- author/source name
- asset format and conversion need
- intended family form
- terrain mask relationship
- interaction/collider role
- proof screenshot or video target

## Edge Cases

- A free asset is not actually compatible with runtime promotion.
- A realistic scan breaks the toon art direction.
- A good model is too heavy for browser/mobile budgets.
- Asset shape does not match terrain raycast placement.
- Asset works locally but public proof has missing files.
- Asset family duplicates an existing protokit role instead of improving readability.

## Research Output

Before implementation, this family needs a candidate ledger row, a protokit contract, a source-mask placement rule, and a human-view proof target.
