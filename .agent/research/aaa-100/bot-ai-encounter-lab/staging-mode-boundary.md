# Staging Mode Boundary

Status: active

## Mode Boundaries

| Mode | Humans | Bots | Allowed claim | Reward/stat policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| practice-yard | 1 | 0-6 | input and interaction practice | no progression |
| solo-bot-run | 1 | 8-24 | solo staging with bots | no competitive stats |
| simulated-20 | 1-4 | fill to 20 | staged 20-body pressure | no competitive stats |
| simulated-60 | 1-4 | fill to 60 | simulated 60-body scale | no live 60-player claim |
| public-bot-proof | 1-4 | configured | public Pages bot proof | proof only |
| future-live-private | 2-60 | optional | live private match | future policy |

## Rule

The UI, reports, and results must show when a run includes bots or simulated bodies. Staging should improve design confidence, not hide the absence of live network authority.
