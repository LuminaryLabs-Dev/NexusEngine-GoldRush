# Restart Policy

Status: active

## Restart With New Knowledge

If a bot implementation attempt fails, restart from the smallest layer that still preserves the end-to-end loop. Do not pile exceptions into the renderer.

## Restart Order

1. Proof boundary labels.
2. Role taxonomy.
3. Roster fixture.
4. Terrain movement.
5. Route intent.
6. Objective behavior.
7. Threat telegraph.
8. Cover behavior.
9. Weapon timing.
10. Encounter director.

## Reset Requirements

Any restart must preserve sanitized reports, local/public proof separation, and the rule that bot proof is staging proof unless real live humans are present.
