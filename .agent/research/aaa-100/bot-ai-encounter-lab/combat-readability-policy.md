# Combat Readability Policy

Status: active

## Policy

Bot combat must be readable before it is hard. Damage is invalid when the player did not receive a fair cue window.

## Required Cues

- Threat direction.
- Weapon intent.
- Cover or exposed body posture.
- Audio cue or dust cue.
- Minimum reaction window.
- Result receipt that names the cause in player-facing language.

## Screenshot States

- Scout visible at distance.
- Bot moving to cover.
- Bot peeking.
- Bot aiming.
- Bot shot missed.
- Bot shot hit.
- Player cashout contested.
- Results with combat pressure summarized.

## Failure

If the player is damaged by a bot before a visible or audible cue is present, the encounter proof fails even if the event receipts are correct.
