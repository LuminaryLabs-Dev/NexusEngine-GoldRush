# Bot Weapon Engagement Source Research

Status: planned

## Reference Signals

- Apex modes: Bot Royale and Private Match show that bot and private contexts can be large while still having separate progression/stat rules.
- PUBG overview: Battle royale pressure is route, loot, survival, and shrinking map pressure, not only combat count.
- PUBG custom match notes: AI can fill custom matches and let a match start with very few humans, but results still need clear presentation.
- Fortnite support: Fill and squad-size choices are explicit player-facing settings.
- GitHub engine collections: Use engine-feature breadth as a checklist for missing systems; do not build a new general engine.

## GoldRush Reading

Define staged weapon engagement with aim, miss, hit, reload, and disengage timings. The source implication is that this system must become a named kit surface with data, events, snapshot, reset, and proof labels.
