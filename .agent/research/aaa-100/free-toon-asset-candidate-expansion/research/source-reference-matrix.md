# Source Reference Matrix

Status: active docs-only
Checked: 2026-07-01

## Purpose

Record the current web-checked sources used by the free toon asset candidate expansion.

## Matrix

| Source | URL | Useful for | Evidence captured | Default decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kenney support | https://kenney.nl/support | general Kenney asset license policy | asset pages described as public domain CC0 and commercial use allowed | strong default candidate |
| Kenney Impact Sounds | https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds | impact, foley, mining, hit, cashout cues | 130 audio files and CC0 license visible | strong first audio candidate |
| Quaternius catalog | https://quaternius.com/ | low-poly toon-compatible models, characters, trains, nature, props | catalog lists train, nature, character, weapon, building, and survival-style packs | strong default candidate with item-level capture |
| Quaternius Modular Train Pack | https://quaternius.com/packs/modulartrain.html | train and tracks | page states train assets and free personal/commercial use | strong first train candidate |
| Quaternius Ultimate Modular Men Pack | https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html | prospector-like character base and animations | page states 11 characters, 24 animations, glTF/FBX/OBJ/Blend, free personal/commercial use | strong character candidate |
| Poly Pizza Modular Train Pack | https://poly.pizza/bundle/Modular-Train-Pack-jYEybkFVr1 | GLTF train delivery path | page lists GLTF download and CC0 per train/track item | strong delivery candidate if download remains direct |
| KayKit Adventurers | https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers | toon characters and GLTF/FBX pipeline | page states CC0 use and engine-compatible formats | strong fallback character candidate |

| Pixabay content license summary | https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/ | Pixabay license check | summary allows free use/adaptation but has prohibited uses | fallback only with project approval |

## Research Conclusion

Best first candidate set: Quaternius/Poly Pizza train or Kenney/Quaternius desert rock/plant. Best first audio set: Kenney Impact Sounds. Best first character set: Quaternius modular humanoid plus KayKit fallback.
