## Shared Requirements

- All reports are sanitized by default.
- Negative fixtures prove unsafe promotion is blocked.
- Positive fixtures prove one valid asset can move one phase forward.
- Browser proof is required for visual or audible runtime assets.
- Public proof is required after promotion.
