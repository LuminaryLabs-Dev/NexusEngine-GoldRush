# Economy Restart Policy

Status: active docs-only

## Restart Triggers

- A reward can be created without a source receipt.
- A persistent reward can be granted from an ineligible mode.
- Practice, bot fill, simulated, private, and public runs share unclear result language.
- Economy numbers move without a tuning decision record.
- Progression blocks the first-run loop or crowds hero controls.
- Cosmetic identity leaks unapproved asset assumptions.
- Public proof uses a stale economy version.

## Restart Action

Create a small local GoldRush kit or adapter that owns the missing boundary. Do not patch economy state through the renderer or result screen only.
