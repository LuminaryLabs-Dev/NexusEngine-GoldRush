# Extraction Stakes Contract

Status: active docs-only

## Purpose

Make extraction the economic heart of GoldRush instead of a generic completion trigger.

## Stakes

- Safe extraction banks carried gold.
- Contested extraction can increase score but adds interruption risk.
- Failed extraction can drop cargo, lose cargo, or mark a recovery window depending on mode.
- Practice and staging modes can explain stakes without granting competitive rewards.
- Results must show what was mined, carried, dropped, lost, banked, and modified.

## Proof

A future implementation should prove at least five outcomes: clean cashout, contested cashout, interrupted cashout, failed route, and recovered cargo.
