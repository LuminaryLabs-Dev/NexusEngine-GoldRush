# Progression Without Grind - Player Loop

Status: planned docs-only
System: 11
Domain: progression/product/ux

## Player Question

What can I earn that helps identity or mastery without turning the game into chores?

## Acceptance

- The player can see why the system matters.
- The result screen explains the outcome.
- Practice/staging/live eligibility is clear.
- The system does not crowd the first-screen hero action.
- The system has a matching receipt and replay summary.

## Human-View Proof

Results and lobby show optional unlock progress without blocking Play/Practice/Run Again.
