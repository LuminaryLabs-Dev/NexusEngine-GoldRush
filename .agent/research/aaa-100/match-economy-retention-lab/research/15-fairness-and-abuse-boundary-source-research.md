## Signal

Fortnite exposes mode, ranked toggle, fill, and team-size options at the player-facing layer. GoldRush needs party size, practice, fill/bot, and competitive-readiness choices to be product contracts, not hidden debug toggles.

## GoldRush Reading

Competitive and ranked modes in battle royale products require stronger integrity than practice modes; GoldRush must label the boundary.
