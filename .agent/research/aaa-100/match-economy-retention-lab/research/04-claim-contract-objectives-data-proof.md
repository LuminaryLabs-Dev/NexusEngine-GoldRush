## Data And Proof

- Data seed: contract id, objective type, route target, value promise, risk tier, completion rule, failure rule, team state.
- Event seed: contract.offered, contract.accepted, contract.progressed, contract.contested, contract.completed, contract.failed.
- Validator target: Scenario proof verifies each contract can start, progress, fail, complete, and summarize without hidden helpers..
- Human-view proof: Human-view proof shows contract selection, route, progress, contest, completion, and result recap..
- Public proof must include the economy/progression ruleset id when the behavior is deploy-facing.
