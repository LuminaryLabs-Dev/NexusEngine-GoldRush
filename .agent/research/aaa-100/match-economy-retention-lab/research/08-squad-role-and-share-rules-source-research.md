Status: planned docs-only

## Signal

PUBG frames battle royale around land, loot, survive, shrinking zone pressure, care package risk, vehicles, training, and solo/duo/squad modes. GoldRush should translate that into train entry, claim route selection, gold/tool/resource economy, final-rush pressure, extraction risk, and staging practice.

## GoldRush Reading

Fortnite exposes Solo, Duo, Trio, and Squad choices; GoldRush group type needs equally clear player-facing meaning.
