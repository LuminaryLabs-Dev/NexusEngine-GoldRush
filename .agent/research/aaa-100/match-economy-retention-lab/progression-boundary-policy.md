# Progression Boundary Policy

Status: active docs-only

## Purpose

Prevent GoldRush from adding grind before the extraction loop is fun, and prevent practice proof from being described as competitive progression.

## Rules

- Practice may teach and summarize, but should not grant competitive stats.
- Bot fill may grant limited learning progress only if labeled.
- Simulated 60-player proof is not live 60-player progression.
- Private rooms should be configurable and labeled separately.
- Cosmetics must not imply gameplay power.
- Tool unlocks must not create pay-to-win or progression-wall behavior.
- Every progression award needs a mode eligibility receipt.

## First Implementation Gate

Before adding long-term unlocks, prove mine/carry/cashout/combat/results are fun and legible without progression pressure.
