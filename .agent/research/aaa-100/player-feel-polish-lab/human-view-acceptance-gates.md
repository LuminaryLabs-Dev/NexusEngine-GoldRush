# Human View Acceptance Gates

Status: active docs-only

## Purpose

Define the player-view states required before a feel axis can be called improved.

## Required Screenshots

- title: identity, readable action, no debug-first surface.
- lobby: 3D character, party intent, leader action, no oversized mode cards.
- loading-yard: train approach, door, board prompt, path readability.
- gold-field idle: player on terrain, horizon readable, no floating debug surfaces.
- movement: camera-relative direction and terrain grounding readable.
- mining: selected resource, hold progress, tool/body/audio/VFX cue.
- cargo: visible carried gold and posture change.
- threat: warning, direction, cover option.
- cashout: landmark, in-range prompt, hold progress, contest risk.
- results: extracted value, risk, replay moments, next action.

## Required Videos

- camera/mouse movement.
- WASD and terrain movement.
- train boarding/departure.
- mine -> carry -> cashout.
- threat/cover/combat.

## Failure State

If a player cannot describe what happened and what to do next from the capture alone, the axis remains unresolved.
