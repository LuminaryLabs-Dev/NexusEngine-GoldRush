# VFX And Diegetic Cues - Source Research

Status: planned docs-only
Axis: 15
Source: GitHub game engine collection
URL: https://github.com/collections/game-engines

## Signal

The collection highlights engines as multi-platform game frameworks. GoldRush should not become a general engine, but its local kits still need engine-like surfaces: render, input, audio, physics, tooling, validation, content, and release gates.

## GoldRush Reading

PUBG and Apex rely on mode-specific player information; GoldRush should provide information diegetically when possible.
