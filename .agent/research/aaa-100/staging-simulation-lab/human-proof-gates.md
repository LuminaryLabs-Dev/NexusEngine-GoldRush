# Human Proof Gates

Status: active docs-only

## Required Screenshot States

- title mode selection.
- lobby party and character identity.
- loading-yard train boarding.
- practice or staging route start.
- resource discovery.
- mining hold.
- cargo carry.
- threat telegraph.
- cover or escape counterplay.
- cashout site.
- extraction result.
- final results/replay.

## Required Video States

Use video only when motion matters:

- mouse-look and WASD direction.
- terrain grounding.
- train timing.
- bot movement.
- combat pressure timing.
- extraction interruption timing.

## Acceptance Rule

A staging proof is not enough if the player cannot tell what happened from the captured view.
