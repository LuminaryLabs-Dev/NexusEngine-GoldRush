## Reference Observation

Hunt foregrounds bounty value, fighting through extraction, and meaningful loss. GoldRush needs carried gold, claim pressure, and cashout to create greed, retreat, and interruption decisions.

## GoldRush Gap

The loop can mine, carry, and cash out structurally, but the stakes need to be felt minute-to-minute through cargo weight, visibility, noise, contest risk, and result consequences.

## Kit Implications

- cargo owns weight, value, visibility, and noise
- extraction owns timer, contest, deposit, and interruption
- results owns what was risked, lost, saved, and learned

## Evidence Required Before Calling This Resolved

- receipt chain for mined, carried, contested, extracted, lost, and banked gold
- human-view proof of why the player would keep mining or leave
- results proof showing loss and recovery clearly

## Edge Cases

- cashout without loss feels like a checkpoint
- loss without readable cause feels unfair
- too much penalty discourages experimentation in single-player staging
